About the programme

What Is Second Bell?

Second Bell is a school health and wellbeing programme by RECOUP Health. It helps school leadership understand how physiological and psychological wellbeing shape attention, learning endurance, and academic performance, then act while there is still time.

In plain terms

What Second Bell does for a school

The programme connects classroom observation, on-campus screening, and the right next step, so leadership is not left guessing about health-related barriers to learning.

  • Understand student health and wellbeing

    See how posture, sleep, stress, screen habits, and physical readiness show up in the school day.

  • Identify concerns that may affect learning

    Non-invasive screening on campus helps flag concerns that can affect learning and participation early.

  • Connect students with the right support

    When a need is found, the school receives guidance on next steps, including specialist support where appropriate.

Programme pillars

What the school programme includes

Four connected parts, delivered on campus and planned around the week the school already runs.

  1. 01

    Preventive health screening

    On-campus checks covering EPHR mapping, postural and biomechanical review, and vision and basic hearing.

  2. 02

    Health education workshops

    Sessions for students, teachers, and parents on ergonomics, nutrition for brain development, and navigating puberty.

  3. 03

    Teacher training

    Help teachers recognise possible concerns without expecting them to diagnose health conditions.

  4. 04

    Specialist support when needed

    A clear route to paediatric specialists, child psychologists, and speech or occupational therapists when screening shows a need.

Designed for school leadership

No disruption to academics. Minimal administrative burden. Schools retain full control of decisions. Second Bell is not a replacement for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.
