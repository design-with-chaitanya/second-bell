Questions and answers

What is Second Bell?

Second Bell is a school health and wellbeing programme by RECOUP Health. It combines preventive screening, health education, teacher training, and specialist support when needed.

Who can enrol a school?

School owners, principals, and leadership teams. Parents and students cannot enrol a school on their own, though parents may share the programme with leadership.

Will it disrupt academics?

No. Second Bell is planned around the existing timetable. The school keeps control of decisions, and reporting stays light.

What is the RECOUP School Health Score?

The RSHS brings screening observations into one score leadership can use, with campus insights and domain-wise analysis. Overview marks on the site are examples, not results from a named school.

Is a classroom observation a diagnosis?

No. Classroom observations and screening help a school look more closely. They do not replace diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

How do we start?

Contact the RECOUP Health team by phone, email, WhatsApp, or the enquiry form. The first step is a consultation about culture, priorities, and timetable fit.
