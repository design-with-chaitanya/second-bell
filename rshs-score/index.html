RSHS

RECOUP School Health Score

The RSHS brings screening observations into one score leadership can use. Individual scores can be read together, so a school sees campus trends and can aim support.

The program overview shows the score mark at 85. That figure is not a measured result of a named school. This page shows the published campus example, 84 out of 100.

How a score is read

The example, 84, sits in the excellent range.

84 marked on a 0–100 scale

80–100

Excellent

Optimal physical and psychological readiness to learn.

60–79

Moderate

Minor issues. Preventive workshops are recommended.

Under 60

Needs attention

Significant barriers to learning. Targeted clinical intervention is required.

What leadership receives

The first screening comes with a short set of deliverables.

  1. Campus insights

    A view of campus health trends and aggregated wellbeing.

  2. Domain-wise analysis

    How the school sits across the six clinical domains.

  3. Early risk identification

    Emerging physiological or psychological barriers, flagged before they escalate.

  4. Actionable recommendations

    Structured next steps written for school leadership.
