RECOUP School Health Score

Understand your school's health and wellbeing picture

The RECOUP School Health Score (RSHS) brings screening observations together into one score out of 100. Individual results are read together, so school leadership can see campus-wide trends and decide where support is most needed.

Example campus score

Example score: 84 out of 100.

An illustrative example of how a campus result is shown. It is not the result of a named school. The homepage overview uses 85 in the same way.

How a score is read

Scores fall into three ranges. The example score of 84 sits in the excellent range.

80–100
ExcellentStudents show strong physical and emotional readiness to learn.
60–79
ModerateSome minor issues. Preventive workshops are recommended.
Under 60
Needs attentionSignificant barriers to learning. Targeted clinical support is needed.

What leadership receives

The first screening comes with a short, practical set of deliverables.

  • Campus insights

    An overview of health and wellbeing trends across the school.

  • Domain-by-domain analysis

    How the school is doing across the six domains of the framework.

  • Early risk identification

    Possible physical or emotional barriers to learning, flagged before they grow.

  • Practical recommendations

    Clear, structured next steps written for school leadership.

About the score: the RSHS describes the school's overall health and wellbeing picture to help leadership plan support. It is not a diagnosis for any individual student.
