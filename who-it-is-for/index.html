Audience

Is Second Bell right for your school?

Second Bell is designed for schools that want a structured approach to student health and wellbeing. Enrolment is a leadership decision. Parents and students cannot enrol a school on their own.

Discuss Your School's Needs See How Enrolment Works

A good fit

Schools that benefit most

Second Bell may be a good fit for leadership teams that want earlier visibility into health-related barriers to learning.

  • School owners and management
  • Principals and school leadership teams
  • Schools looking for preventive health screening on campus
  • Schools interested in teacher and parent health education
  • Schools seeking a structured way to understand student wellbeing needs

How enrolment works

  1. School leadership starts the conversation.
  2. An initial consultation covers needs, priorities, and timetable.
  3. Together, you decide whether the programme suits your school.

If you are a parent, you are welcome to share Second Bell with your school's leadership. Enrolment remains a school decision.

Who is involved once a school enrols

  • Leadership

    Sets culture fit, timetable boundaries, and keeps control of decisions throughout.

  • Teachers

    Learn to recognise everyday signals without being asked to diagnose health conditions.

  • Students and parents

    Join workshops and receive clearer routes to support when screening finds a need.
