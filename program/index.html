The school programme

What Second Bell includes

Second Bell brings preventive health work onto campus. Screening, education, teacher training, and specialist support fit into the week your school already runs.

  • No disruption to academics
  • Minimal administrative burden
  • School leadership stays in control
01

Preventive health screening

Non-invasive checks carried out on campus, so small issues can be noticed before they affect learning.

  • EPHR mapping
  • Postural and biomechanical checks
  • Vision and basic hearing checks
02

Health education workshops

Practical sessions for students, teachers, and parents, so healthy habits continue beyond the school gate.

  • Ergonomics and posture
  • Nutrition for brain development
  • Navigating puberty
03

Teacher training

Helps teachers recognise possible concerns, such as telling a distracted student from a tired one, without expecting them to diagnose.

  • Learning differences
  • Speech and motor delays
  • Behavioural health first aid
04

Specialist support when appropriate

A clear route to further assessment when screening shows that a student may need more help.

  • Paediatric specialists
  • Child psychologists
  • Speech and occupational therapists

Who takes part

  • School leadership decides how the programme fits the school.
  • Teachers learn to understand what they already notice in class.
  • Students, teachers, and parents take part in the workshops.
  • Specialists are involved when screening shows a need.

What the school receives

After baseline screening, leadership receives the first RECOUP School Health Score, with campus insights, a domain-by-domain analysis, early identification of possible risks, and practical recommendations.
