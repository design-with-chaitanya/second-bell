How it works

Getting started is simple

Schools are busy, structured places. Second Bell is designed to add health support without taking over the school day. There are three phases, and the school stays in charge of each one.

  • No disruption to academics
  • Minimal administrative burden
  • School leadership stays in control

From the first conversation to the school routine

  1. 1Phase 1

    Step 1: Initial discussion

    A conversation with school leadership about your school's culture, priorities, timetable, and the health measures already in place.

  2. 2Phase 2

    Step 2: Baseline assessment

    Structured, non-invasive screening on campus, planned around classes. It covers posture, biomechanics, developmental markers, and readiness to learn.

  3. 3Phase 2

    Step 3: Understand the findings

    Leadership receives the first RECOUP School Health Score, with campus insights, a domain-by-domain view, and early identification of possible risks.

  4. 4Phase 3

    Step 4: Plan the next steps

    Agree on recommendations together. Workshops, teacher guidance, and specialist support then become part of the school routine, with light reporting.

The three phases in more detail

Phase 1

Alignment and consultation

It begins with school leadership. The conversation covers the school's culture, academic pressures, and the health measures already in place, so ongoing academics are not disrupted.

Phase 2

Baseline screening and the first score

Structured, non-invasive screening on campus, followed by the school's first RECOUP School Health Score. Baseline notes cover posture, biomechanics, developmental markers, and readiness to learn.

About the RSHS score
Phase 3

Routine integration

Workshops, teacher guidance, and specialist support become part of the school week. Reporting stays light, and decisions remain with the school.
