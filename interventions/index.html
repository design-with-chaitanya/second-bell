Interventions

Targeted support when screening finds a need

These supports follow a concern that screening has already highlighted. Each is matched to the student's physical or emotional need, and together they form one connected set.

  • 01

    Speech & Language

    Support for articulation, stuttering, and delays in understanding or using language that affect class participation and social confidence.

  • 02

    Occupational Therapy

    Support for fine motor skills, handwriting, sensory processing, and executive functioning, such as planning and organising work.

  • 03

    Behavioural Health

    Support for anxiety, ADHD symptoms, difficulty managing emotions, and severe exam stress, with the focus on coping rather than punishment.

  • 04

    RH SPRINT

    Sports health. Biomechanical screening for active students, aimed at preventing injuries before they take a student out of play.

  • 05

    Digital Balance

    Guidance on screen time, digital fatigue, and protecting the sleep that learning depends on.

  • 06

    Teacher Training

    Ongoing support on learning differences, neurodiversity, and adapting the classroom after screening.

Good to know: Teacher training is also one of the four parts of the core programme. Here it continues as ongoing support after screening.
