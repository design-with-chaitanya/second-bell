Interventions

When screening finds a need

These supports are specific. They follow a risk screening has already highlighted, matched to the physiological or psychological need in front of the student. They are one set, not six separate programs.

  1. 01

    Speech & Language

    Articulation, stuttering, and expressive or receptive language delays that get in the way of class participation and social confidence.

  2. 02

    Occupational Therapy

    Fine motor skill, handwriting, sensory processing, and executive functioning. The coordination the school day quietly asks for.

  3. 03

    Behavioral Health

    Anxiety, ADHD symptoms, emotional dysregulation, and severe exam stress. Coping, ahead of punishment.

  4. 04

    RH SPRINT

    Sports health. Biomechanical screening, before an injury takes a student out of play.

  5. 05

    Digital Balance

    Screen time, digital fatigue, and the sleep that learning depends on.

  6. 06

    Teacher Training

    Learning differences, neurodiversity, and classroom adaptation. Teacher training is also a pillar of the school program. Here it is the continuing intervention.
