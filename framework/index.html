Framework

Six domains of observation

Second Bell sorts student wellbeing into six clinical domains. A difficulty in one place, such as poor sleep, often travels into another, such as emotional dysregulation or a shorter attention span.

These names are not the six school-day signals. Attention and readiness, emotional regulation, student fatigue, digital habits, posture and physicality, and student wellbeing stay a separate list.

Physical Readiness Attention & Effort Emotional Balance Digital & Lifestyle Adolescent Health Sports Safety
01

Physical Readiness

Posture, core stamina, and whether a student can sit and focus without pain.

Poor posture can shallow breathing and bring fatigue forward by mid-morning.

02

Attention & Effort

Cognitive bandwidth after strain, sleep debt, and sensory overload.

Chronic fatigue hurts sustained attention.

03

Emotional Balance

Stress and dysregulation that look like behavior.

Unrecognized stress is often read as defiance.

04

Digital & Lifestyle

Screens, broken sleep, and sedentary hours, and what they do to academic performance.

Late-night device use can cut REM sleep and memory consolidation.

05

Adolescent Health

Puberty. Hormonal change needs structured, empathetic guidance, not discipline alone.

06

Sports Safety

Biomechanical screening to reduce repeat injury.

Early faults can prevent later chronic joint pain.
